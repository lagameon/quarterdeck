How Quarterdeck compares

There are several good ways to reach a coding agent from your phone. They make different trade-offs; this page says where Quarterdeck sits and when another tool is the better choice.

The other columns summarise what each project's own README said on 27 November 2026. Projects move fast — check theirs before deciding, and tell us if something here is out of date.

Choose Quarterdeck if…

  • you already use Tailscale, or want nothing between your phone and your machine but your own network;
  • you want to keep starting Claude Code the way you do now and see those sessions, including the real terminal;
  • you work across several machines, or with more than one Claude account;
  • you want something small you can read and change.

Choose something else if…

  • you use Codex, OpenCode or other agents — Happy, Paseo, CloudCLI and HAPI all support several;
  • you want a native app, voice, or a polished multi-agent workspace;
  • you don't want to run Tailscale, or you want a hosted service that works without setting anything up.
